IDENT OS's default licence for original code **whose copyright holders have authorised MIT licensing** is the [MIT License](../LICENSE).

## Existing contributions and historical licence

Earlier project revisions were published under Apache License 2.0. That licence remains applicable to historical releases and to any contribution not validly relicensed by its copyright owner. The full historical licence is preserved in [LICENSES/Apache-2.0.txt](../LICENSES/Apache-2.0.txt).

Changing the repository's top-level licence does not retroactively revoke Apache-2.0 grants, transfer copyright, or automatically relicense contributions from other owners. Maintainers must audit provenance and obtain any required permissions before representing pre-existing contributions as MIT-only. When in doubt, preserve the existing Apache-2.0 notice on that file.

## Third-party components

IDENT OS uses Debian GNU/Linux, the Linux kernel, BusyBox, XFCE, GNU tools, and other packages under their own licences. These are **not** relicensed under MIT by this repository. A built ISO is a distribution of multiple separately licensed components, including GPL-covered software. Distributors must preserve notices, comply with applicable source and corresponding-source requirements, and review the exact packages shipped.

The IDENT name and branding are not licensed as trademarks by the MIT licence. The licence gives copyright permissions for covered code, not endorsement rights.

For package provenance and licence issues, raise an issue without disclosing sensitive details or follow the security policy.
